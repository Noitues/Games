# Roster v1.4.0 (rules 1.0.0)

Refit to the RQ-030 recalibration of Rules 14.2: credits cut to -1.5 per AP and -1 per cooldown round, and every ability must net at least 4 because it spends a whole activation. Identities, icons and roles are unchanged; only numbers moved.
