Rules quick reference

Each generation: Growth → Commit → Fast → Dusk → Day → Dawn → Slow → Time passes.

Leaders: your nominee is free and fast and may be voted heir. Heir (Dawn) → Steward (Day: Reign, then its event Grows) → Elder (Dusk: Legacy in its event's sections, then its event Collapses) → the Wall.

Events name section types. Seed: +1 Glitch in each matching section on the Outer Hull or next to a Fault/Failure. Grow: +1 Fault in each matching section with threat (Failure if it has 2+ Faults). Collapse: Glitch 1 + Fault 2 + Failure 3 damage (+Unrest, −Brace, −Isolate); 2+ places Decay; the same damage hits Crew (2 health each); surviving Crew deal 2 damage each.

Decay comes from the pool; each house loses 1 influence there; Decay on Decay cascades to a neighbor. Pool empty: Ship in Crisis flips; empty again: you lose.

Purpose: destroying a Fault +1, a Failure +2. A full pool earns a Discovery (apply now, or Breakthrough on the Steward). Every 3 Discoveries raise Understanding.

Win at any moment: I no threat and no Decay · II no threat · III no Faults or Failures (plus any destination requirement). Lose: events run out, Decay runs out after the crisis, or a house has no influence.

Tokens: Brace X prevents X damage · Quarantine: nothing seeds, vents or cascades in or out · Patch skips the next Grow · Lockout skips the next Seed · Unrest +1 damage from every source · Isolate negates damage at the next Collapse.

Range counts from sections with your influence. Elements come from the cards you play (and track slots); they power card bonuses and your innate.
