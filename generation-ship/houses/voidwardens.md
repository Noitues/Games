# House Voidwardens, the Airlock Keepers

**Template:** Ocean's Hungry Grasp · **Curve:** front-loaded (1.43)

Masters of the Outer Hull. Threat they vent out of a Hull section is thrown into the void, and they earn credits for big pieces.

- **Guilds:** Navigators (primary, 1 PP) / Engineers (secondary, 1.5 PP)
- **Weaknesses:** Can only add influence to Outer Hull sections; weak in the Core.

## Special rule
Threat the Voidwardens vent out of an Outer Hull section goes into the void (destroyed, no Purpose); gain 1 credit per Fault or Failure voided. Voidwardens can only add influence to Outer Hull sections.
- *Estimated value:* ≈ 1.5 PP per generation.
