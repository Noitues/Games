# House Keel, the Hullwrights

**Template:** Vital Strength of the Earth · **Curve:** steep

Slow, sturdy guardians of the ship's structure and its people. Every section they hold is braced at Dusk.

- **Guilds:** Medics (primary) / Laborers (secondary)
