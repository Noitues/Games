# Hex-Nexus playtest: quick start

**Build on the table:** Rules 1.9.0 · Roster 1.9.0 · Config `batch_0075`
(death timers 2 / 4 / 6). The panel (top right) always shows the build. Put it
in any feedback you send.

## Seats and setup (5 minutes)

1. **Seats:** North plays **Blue**, South plays **Red**. Use **First: Blue/Red**
   on the panel to set who has priority in round 1. Priority alternates every
   round after that.
2. **Draft:** from your champion deck, take one card per role: Top, Jungle,
   Mid, ADC, Support. Put the cards in the **0 · HAND** slot of your cooldown
   track.
3. **Standees:** search your champion bag for the matching standees and put
   all five on your **fountain** (the base tile).
4. **Chips:** take 3 neutral chips into your **AP POOL**. The panel counts
   them.
5. The board starts with every hexgroup **hidden**, towers and Nexus at full
   HP, and the jungle camps up. The Dragon and Baron show *down* until their
   round.

## Each round (the panel walks you through it with Next phase ▶)

1. **Upkeep**, in this order:
   - press *Next round*;
   - shift every card on both cooldown tracks down 1; cards reaching 0 return
     to hand, and dead champions respawn at the fountain;
   - fountain heal;
   - structure decay, when the panel says so;
   - reset each AP pool to 3 chips;
   - waves move 2, Player 1's first;
   - spawn waves when the panel says *waves spawn +N*;
   - monster respawns;
   - flip back any hexgroup holding only one team.
2. **Action**, in snake order **1 2 2 1 1 2 2 1 1 2** (1 = the priority player):
   - activate one champion from hand: move up to its Speed, and use at most one
     ability before or after the move;
   - using an ability puts the **whole card** on the track at that ability's
     cooldown;
   - a chip a champion knocks off a wave or monster goes **straight into your
     AP pool**;
   - structure chips go back to the supply.
3. **World:** waves hit adjacent enemies for 1; towers hit champions for 2 and
   waves for 1; monsters hit adjacent champions for 1.
4. **Shop:** Player 1 then Player 2 spend leftover AP on item cards.
5. **Win check:** a Nexus at 0 loses. At the round limit, compare towers
   destroyed, then structure HP, then kills.

## Table controls

| Want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Change HP | Left-click the counter on a standee, tower, Nexus or monster: −1. Right-click: +1. |
| Flip a hexgroup | Right-click the tile → **Flip hexgroup**. |
| Add chips | Drag them out of the Blue, Red or neutral chip bags (infinite). |
| Kill a champion | Standee off the board; card on the track at the death position the panel shows (3 / 5 / 7 = 2 / 4 / 6 rounds missed). Take the **kill AP** the panel shows (6 / 8 / 10). |
| Take a tower | Its killer's team gains 3 AP. Protection: T1 before T2, and one full lane before the Nexus. |
| Hide the panel | Click **HN**. |

## Hidden hexgroups: the short version

- A hidden tile is **one space** for movement. Ranges are still counted in
  hexes.
- Your champion inside a hidden tile can't be targeted from outside. An enemy
  champion **within 2 hexes** reveals the tile.
- A tile with both teams in it must be face up.

The mod does not conceal standees yet: be honest about where you are.
