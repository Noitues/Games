# How to Play

You play one character in a story set in a drowning marshland. The GM describes the world; you say what your character does.

## Rolling

When the outcome is uncertain, you roll four Fate dice (4dF), each showing −1, 0 or +1, and add your skill. Compare the total to the opposition on the ladder:

+8 Legendary, +7 Epic, +6 Fantastic, +5 Superb, +4 Great, +3 Good, +2 Fair, +1 Average, +0 Mediocre, −1 Poor, −2 Terrible

Never describe dice results yourself — the table's dice are rolled for you.

**Outcomes**

- **Fail** (below the opposition): you don't get what you wanted. You may instead choose success at a major cost.
- **Tie:** success at a minor cost.
- **Success** (1–2 above): you get what you wanted.
- **Success with style** (3 or more above): you get what you wanted, plus something extra.

## Your theme cards

Your character is written on four theme cards: two ROOTS (the origins, people and places you hold on to) and two REACH (your oaths, burdens, powers and goals). Each card has three power tags and one weakness tag.

At the start of the session one of your cards is set aside face-down and isn't available to you until the story brings it back. The GM draws complications from a face-down Session Deck on ties, on major costs and at some scene twists.

## Invoking

Name a live tag that fits what you're doing and spend 1 fate point or a free invoke. Gain +2 to the roll, or reroll all four dice. Each tag can be invoked once per roll.

## Fate points and compels

You start with 3 fate points.

When a weakness or other trouble would complicate your life, the GM may offer a compel. Accept it and you gain 1 fate point and the complication happens. Refuse it and you pay 1 fate point. You can't refuse with 0 fate points.

## Harm

You have two stress tracks, physical and mental, with 2 boxes each. Check a box to absorb a hit. For bigger hits, take a consequence: mild (absorbs 2), moderate (absorbs 4) or severe (absorbs 6). A consequence is a new aspect describing the injury, and it also makes one of your cards temporarily unavailable. If you can't absorb a hit, you're taken out and your opponent decides what happens to you.

**Conceding:** before a roll in a conflict, you may concede. You lose, but you decide how, and you gain 1 fate point plus 1 for each consequence you took in that conflict.

## Your past

The GM may ask you questions about your character's past. Your answers become true.
