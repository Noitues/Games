# Fate (baseline) — GM rules

Standard Fate. Characters have a high concept, a trouble and three aspects, and Fate Core skills.

- **Rolls:** 4dF + skill against opposition on the ladder (+8 Legendary … +1 Average, 0 Mediocre, −1 Poor, −2 Terrible). Passive opposition starts at Average (+1), +1 for each table aspect that directly opposes the action, to a maximum of Great (+4). Named NPCs roll 4dF + their rating as active opposition.
- **Outcomes:** fail (the player may take success at a major cost); tie = success at a minor cost; 1–2 = success; 3+ = success with style. The GM invents costs.
- **Aspects and invokes:** name a live aspect that fits, spend 1 fate point or a free invoke: +2 or reroll. Each aspect once per roll. Situation aspects on the table can be invoked by anyone if the fiction supports it. Create an advantage makes a new situation aspect with 1 free invoke (2 on success with style).
- **Fate points:** players start the session with 3 (their refresh), or more if they already have more. The GM gets 1 fate point per player at the start of each scene; unspent points don't carry over. Invoking a player's trouble against them is a hostile invoke; they gain 1 fate point at the end of the scene.
- **Compels:** the GM may offer a compel on any player aspect or trouble whenever the story calls for it. Accept = +1 fate point and the complication happens; refuse = pay 1 fate point; a player with 0 fate points cannot refuse.
- **Conflict:** attack vs defence; shifts become stress. Two tracks (physical and mental) with boxes worth 1 and 2 (Physique/Will add boxes as in Fate Core). Consequences: mild 2, moderate 4, severe 6. A hit that can't be absorbed takes the character out. Before a roll, a player may concede: they lose on their own terms and gain 1 fate point plus 1 per consequence taken in that conflict.
- **Scenes:** a scene ends when its central question is answered or the party leaves. A mild consequence clears at the end of the next scene.
